Good day everyone, the beautiful interface you see before yourself is of my project HadeS.
A local AI-powered teaching assistant that takes your learning videos and turns them
into an interactive AI tutor. Just think about it, instead of going through hours and hours
of videos just to revise a single concept or clear a doubt, what if you could simply ask
the questions and get the answer directly from the source? One of the biggest advantages of
HadeS is that it runs completely locally, keeping your learning data private. It also
provides exact timestamps for its answers so you do not just have to trust what the AI says,
you can verify it from the source yourself by the embedded player given in HadeS.
For this current demonstration, we have three datasets, a machine learning one, a Python one,
and a quantum computing one. Let's use python for this one. Suppose I want to learn functions,
I will simply ask HadeS a question about them and as you can see HadeS semantically searches
through its vector databases to find the most relevant chunks of their video transcripts.
It retrieves their relevant information and then sends it to our locally running Ollama model
and generates an answer based on the source material, that's why it's grounded and here is the
interesting part. It doesn't just give you an answer, it shows you where the answer came from.
I can click on the source and watch the original video right here, at the exact timestamp where
the information was discussed. To play that video you need an internet connection,
but HadeS itself runs completely offline.
And if I want to test my understanding, I can quiz myself
directly with HadeS. Let's try another question, this time about variables. Once again HadeS retrieves
the relevant information and generates an answer with its sources, making it easier to revise
concepts without manually searching through the entire video but learning doesn't stop at asking
questions. After we have had a discussion, we can ask HadeS to summarize the entire chat and give
us a quick overview of everything we have covered and if we want something that we can revise later,
we have the function of downloading the study guide right here. It basically turns your entire
chat into notes and we can also generate a summary after three questions. So that's HadeS,
a completely local AI teaching assistant that combines video-based learning,
semantic search, timestamp sources, self-quizzing and automatic study notes in one place.
The goal is simple, folks, spend less time searching through the
learning material and more time actually learning. Thank you.
